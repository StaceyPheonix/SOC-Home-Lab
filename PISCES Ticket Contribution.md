## About PISCES International

**PISCES** stands for the **Public Infrastructure Security Cyber Education System**. It is a nonprofit organization dedicated to bridging the cybersecurity talent gap while defending vulnerable public infrastructure.

### Artifact Contribution: Ticket #0008582

This repository includes a practical example of a **PISCES Incident Note** generated during SOC operations. 

* **The Scenario:** I triaged an alert involving anomalous traffic on **Port 445 (SMB Services)**. 
* **The Analysis:** In this investigation I successfully identified a critical spike in traffic (transferring ~890MB of data) and cross-referenced Geo-IP telemetry to flag signs of potential lateral movement, brute-force attempts, or data exfiltration.
* **The Outcome:** The ticket showcases real-world incident response escalation, mapping out immediate isolation protocols and strategic patch management recommendations to protect the affected community infrastructure.
